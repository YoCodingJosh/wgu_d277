Kansas!

Kansas is a state in the Midwestern United States. Its capital is Topeka and its largest city is Wichita. Kansas is named after its river, Kansas River, which is actually named after the Kansa tribe of Native Americans. Source: Kansas Wikipedia page

Kansas' agriculture outputs wheat, grain sorghum, and beef. Source: Kansas Department of Commerce

For more information, visit the Kansas Wikipedia page.
